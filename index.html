Our story
Who is Jacy?
Born in the kitchen,
built for campus

Jacy grew up watching her mother cook moi moi for the whole neighbourhood. Every wrap was made with patience — the right blend of beans, the right heat, the right love. That recipe never left her hands.

When she arrived at UNIZIK, she noticed something was missing. Students were eating whatever was fast and cheap, not what was good. So she started making her moi moi right there on campus — fresh every morning, no shortcuts, no preservatives.

Word spread fast. One order became ten. Ten became a waiting list. Today, Jacy's is the first dedicated moi moi brand on UNIZIK campus — and it is only the beginning.
